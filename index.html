How EZTV works
Cable boxHDMI → Capture cardUSB → Host computerinternet → Your phone or tablet

At home: Host

  1. Plug the capture card into the computer. Use one card per screen (up to 4).
  2. Open EZTV here and tap Host.
  3. Follow steps 1 to 4 and keep the tab open.

Anywhere: Watch

  1. Open the invite link the host sent you.
  2. Send the reply code back to the host.
  3. Watch one screen or several, full screen, with the remote.

Video goes straight from the host to you. Nothing is uploaded to a server and there is no account.

    Help & troubleshooting

    Full screen and multi-screen
    • Full screen: tap Full screen in the control bar. The bar hides itself; tap the screen to bring it back. The Remote button slides the remote over the video.
    • iPhone: Safari can't make a web page truly full screen, so EZTV fills the whole display instead. For the most room, tap Share → Add to Home Screen and open EZTV from the icon. Rotate to landscape for a bigger picture.
    • Android: Chrome goes truly full screen and turns landscape on its own. Install it with ⋮ → Add to Home screen for an app-like view.
    • Several screens: tap a screen to choose it for sound and the remote. Double-tap to focus it, or use Layout to switch between Grid and Focus. Each screen also has its own focus and full screen buttons.
    • Pop out shows the chosen screen in a floating window while you use other apps (where your device supports it).
    My capture card isn't in the list
    • Tap Refresh devices and allow camera and microphone access when asked.
    • Close OBS, Zoom, Teams or other tabs using the card. Only one app can use it at a time.
    • Try another USB port (blue USB 3 ports work best) and unplug and replug the card.
    • Cards usually show up as "USB Video", "HDMI Capture" or "UVC Camera", not your laptop webcam.
    The picture is black
    • Make sure the box is on and its HDMI goes into the capture card's IN port.
    • Some boxes and apps use copy protection (HDCP) and capture cards can't record them. Live cable channels usually work.
    • Try 720p · 30 fps, then Start again.
    No sound
    • On the host, pick the card's sound source (often "USB Digital Audio" or "HDMI Capture"). The level bar should move.
    • On the viewer, tap Sound. Phones start muted. Sound plays from the screen you've selected.
    The viewer can't connect
    • Keep the host tab open and capturing, and use a fresh invite for every connection.
    • Some mobile networks block direct connections. Try Wi-Fi, a VPN to your home network, or add a TURN server in Settings.
    • If the link is too long for your messaging app, send the invite code instead.
    Control my box without an IR blaster
    • No web page can send infrared, so EZTV uses the EZTV Bridge: a small program (bridge.py) you run on the host computer. It sends the buttons to your box over your home network.
    • It works with Roku, DirecTV, Fire TV and Android / Google TV (turn on ADB debugging), and with anything that has a web address to control it (Tasmota, ESPHome, Home Assistant, Harmony).
    • Most cable company boxes (Xfinity, Spectrum, Cox, Optimum) only listen to IR or Bluetooth remotes. For those, a cheap network IR blaster (Broadlink, or an ESP with Tasmota) is the way, and the Bridge talks to it too.
    • HDMI remote control (CEC) doesn't pass through a capture card, so it can't be used here.
    vSeeBox Elite Ultra remote and the UFO-R6
    • Tap vSeeBox Remote at the top. The remote floats over the app. Drag it by its top bar to any corner, double-tap the bar to shrink it, and slide Glass transparency in Setup to see more of the screen behind it.
    • The path is: remote → bridge.py on the host computer → UFO-R6 → infrared → vSeeBox. The browser never sends IR itself, and your Tuya device key stays inside bridge.json, never in this page.
    • Open Setup in the remote, pick a button, tap Learn IR, then press the same key on the original vSeeBox remote while pointing it at the UFO-R6. Do this once per button. Dashed buttons are not learned yet.
    • From a phone in Watch mode, the remote sends the press to the host computer, which fires the IR.
    Remote buttons do nothing
    • On the host, finish step 4: tick Use EZTV Bridge and tap Check connection. It should list your devices.
    • Each screen number needs a device in bridge.json. The viewer's message tells you what went wrong.
    • For a webhook: on https, a plain http:// address on your network is blocked by browsers. Use the Bridge instead, or open EZTV from http://localhost:8787 while the Bridge is running.
    EZTV runs entirely in your browser. No accounts, no uploads.

    Settings

    TURN server (fixes "Connection failed")

    Use this when a phone on cellular data, or another network, can't see the stream. Enter the same TURN details on the host and on every viewer device, then make a fresh invite. Separate several URLs with a space or comma, for example turn:host:80 turns:host:443.

    Leave these blank to use the built-in relay.
